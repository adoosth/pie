# Arabic loans in Persian

Every word below states a borrowing from Arabic in the leading
row of its recorded ancestry. The band is the same for all of them — **non-PIE**,
dark grey — whatever the sound laws can do with the form: the word did not come
from Proto-Indo-European, so the model does not get credit for it.

The contact window (633–1100, Arabic into Persian) is the period in which the two languages were in contact. It is a window, not a date for the word: the dictionary says *that* the word was borrowed and never *when*.

**2,079 words** in the glossary state this borrowing; the table shows the first 120.
